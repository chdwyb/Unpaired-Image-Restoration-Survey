# Access and reuse

This collection links to papers, datasets and implementations hosted by their authors or publishers. Those works retain their respective copyright and license terms. Their inclusion here does not grant permission to redistribute them or use them commercially.

“Code linked” describes the presence of an attributable public implementation link. It does not mean that the repository has an open-source license, that every training component is released, or that results have been reproduced. Consult the linked repository's license, dataset terms and model-weight terms separately.

No paper full text, dataset image, third-party implementation or model weight is redistributed in this collection. Original statistical artwork is generated only from the collection's bibliographic metadata. Any existing repository-level license governs this collection's own material; no third-party license is replaced by that license.
